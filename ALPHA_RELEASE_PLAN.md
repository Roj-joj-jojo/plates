## Likes/comments contract — approved core and recommended limits

The audience/moderation/notification/erasure rules were approved through this session's scope question. Text-only flat comments, Feed-versus-detail placement, pagination order, self-like behaviour and precise lifecycle placeholders are recommended limits to settle before implementation if they affect the owner's intended behaviour.

- Anyone who can currently read the original Plate can like/comment, including readers of an open account who do not follow it. Anonymous users and unapproved readers of private Plates cannot read/write interactions or their counts.
- One reversible like per account/Plate; show count and viewer's current state. No reaction picker, liker directory or like notification in this slice. No special self-like restriction proposed.
- Flat text-only comments, newest comments reachable through stable pagination. Feed offers like/count and a comment count/link; the Plate page hosts the comment list/composer. No nested replies, comment photos or direct-comment mentions in this slice.
- Comment authors can edit/remove their own comments; Plate authors can remove comments on their Plate but cannot rewrite others' text. Use confirmation for removal, revision checks for edits, duplicate-submit protection and in-flight/error states. Removal hides text while retaining required structural context; no physical deletion is authorised by this plan. Exact implementation/removal semantics need owner approval before building.
- Comment audience follows the original Plate's current access. Privacy changes/follower removal deny fresh reads/writes/counts/notification detail to newly ineligible viewers. Already delivered text cannot be recalled. Retained Menu snapshots provide no access to original comments; Table membership alone provides no access either.
- Existing Table-share discussions remain independent private threads. Do not merge their comments/counts into the Plate discussion, or copy direct comments into Menu saves.
- Proposed notification: a new comment by another person notifies the Plate author once; own comments, edits, removal and likes do not. No email/push or notifications to every prior commenter. Opening/read lists must recheck current Plate access and hide inaccessible comment content/context. Table-share activity and Request-reply notifications remain separate backlog work.
- Unpublishing makes the original discussion unavailable; comment removal should not disclose old text. Account erasure must cover authored comment text/attribution, likes and notification records, including restored copies. Exact retained placeholders and erasure attribution require schema review. Ordinary account privacy changes do not erase previously written comments for remaining eligible Plate readers; explain that audience clearly.
- User-facing blocking/reporting remains deferred in the existing backlog. Confirm existing owner controls plus Plate-author moderation are sufficient for the small admitted alpha; account suspension is currently unimplemented.
